# Glossary

Settled meanings of through-the-cracks's project terms: one or two sentences per term, saying what the thing is.

## Language

**Episode**:
A single aired installment of WEVL 89.9 FM's *Through the Cracks* with an air date, optional episode number, title, source post URL, and ordered **Playlist**.
_Avoid_: Show, broadcast

**Playlist**:
The ordered sequence of **Spins** aired during an **Episode**.
_Avoid_: Tracklist, setlist

**Broadcast Log**:
The chronological catalog of aired **Episodes** paired with each selected **Episode**'s **Playlist**.
_Avoid_: Episode feed, show archive

**Song**:
A unique musical work in the catalog, identified by title and **Artist**.
_Avoid_: Track (when referring to unique catalog items)

**Spin**:
A single broadcast airing of a **Song** within an **Episode**, and the canonical count unit across archive metrics and tables.
_Avoid_: Play, song play, hit

**Artist**:
A musician, band, or ensemble credited with performing **Songs** in the archive.
_Avoid_: Performer, act

**Album**:
A recorded release from which a **Song** spin is sourced when credited in the archive.
_Avoid_: Record, LP
